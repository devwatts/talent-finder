The Opportunity




We have an exciting opportunity for a Senior Civil Engineer to join our Perth team. As our project portfolio continues to expand across the resources, energy, and industrial sectors, this role will play a key part in strengthening and growing our civil engineering and design capabilities. This is an excellent opportunity for a motivated and experienced engineer who enjoys technical challenges, values collaboration, and wants to contribute to a high-performing team environment.




Our Civil team delivers engineering solutions across mining, energy, and infrastructure projects, with expertise in heavy haul roads, rail infrastructure, bulk earthworks, drainage and hydrology, water infrastructure, non-process infrastructure (NPI), and construction support. We are recognised for delivering practical, constructible, and cost-effective solutions that optimise project outcomes for our clients.




Key Responsibilities




Deliver complex civil engineering design solutions with minimal supervision.
Prepare and review engineering designs, calculations, technical reports, and supporting documentation.
Interpret and apply relevant industry codes, standards, and client specifications.
Provide technical leadership and guidance to junior engineers and designers.
Review delegated work to ensure quality, accuracy, and compliance with project requirements.
Contribute to project planning, design reviews, and client engagement activities.
