Job Description

The Journeyman Electrician is responsible for the installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, and repair of complex electrical systems within a high-speed manufacturing and production environment.

This role is ideal for a licensed electrician who takes pride in craftsmanship, values safety and structure, and is seeking long-term stability in a professional industrial setting.

Note: This position requires an active Journeyman Electrician license or Journeyman card (Florida or reciprocal state). Candidates without proper certification will not be considered.

Pay rate: $32 - $40 per Hour

Job Responsibilities


Install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair industrial electrical systems and production equipment.
Perform preventative and corrective maintenance to ensure optimal equipment uptime and reliability.
Work with high-voltage systems (up to 480V), including power distribution, switchgear, transformers, and motor controls.
Read and interpret electrical diagrams, schematics, blueprints, and technical drawings.
Bend and install conduit, pull wire, and install electrical fixtures, panels, and controls.
Perform advanced troubleshooting and root cause analysis to resolve electrical faults and minimize downtime.
Test electrical components for voltage, current, continuity, and resistance using appropriate testing equipment.
Utilize CMMS/work order systems (e.g., Maximo) to document maintenance activities and track work orders.
Partner with engineering, maintenance, and production teams on equipment installations, upgrades, and continuous improvement projects.
Collaborate with third-party vendors and contractors as needed.
Maintain strict adherence to site-specific safety policies, including lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedures.
Operate lifts, forklifts, and work safely at heights when required.
Mentor and provide guidance to apprentice electricians or junior team members as assigned.

At Aramark, developing new skills and doing what it takes to get the job done make a positive impact for our employees and for our customers. In order to meet our commitments, job duties may change or new ones may be assigned without formal notice.

Qualifications


Active Journeyman Electrician license (Florida or reciprocal state required).
Minimum 7+ years of commercial and/or industrial electrical experience (manufacturing experience strongly preferred).
High school diploma or equivalent required; technical or trade school certification preferred.
Strong knowledge of National Electrical Code (NEC) and electrical safety standards.
Demonstrated ability to work independently and within a team-oriented environment.
Strong problem-solving, troubleshooting, and diagnostic skills.
Effective communication and documentation skills.
Experience working with industrial equipment, automation systems, or production lines is a plus.
