Description

The Senior Paralegal performs a variety of specialized, articulate, and administrative support duties for multiple Attorney’s and manages the clerical operations of complex prelitigation personal injury cases.

Position Responsibilities:


Case Management
Investigation of all insurance coverages available to the client
Obtaining, reviewing, and organizing medical records to complete a treatment outline
Preparing, calculating, and maintaining damage summaries including medical expenses, lost wages, and property damage
Logging daily mail and drafting any correspondence necessary in the day-to-day case management
Preparation of file for litigation transfer when necessary
Submissions
Preparing and forwarding medical specials to insurance carriers with demand summary
Meeting and exceeding goals set
Communication
corresponding with clients, Attorney and insurance adjusters regarding case status
Consistent client contacts
Weekly reports
Monthly team meetings
Maintain professional interactions, internally and externally

Requirements


Exceptional interpersonal and customer service skills
Strong attention to detail and the ability to multi-task while maintaining organization
Ability to exercise patience and professionalism in dealing with difficult clients and providers.
Strong working knowledge of MS Office, and the ability to operate standard office equipment
Must work well in a team atmosphere
Must maintain excellent attendance and punctuality
High command of English grammar, usage, punctuation, and spelling as well as use of computers and related applications.


Education:


High School Diploma/ GED required
Three to five years’ relevant work experience preferred
Prefer Bachelor’s degree and/or paralegal certificate


Physical Requirements:


Prolonged periods sitting at a desk and working on a computer.
Must be able to lift up to 40 pounds at times.
This job operates in a professional office environment. Exposure to computer screens and low level of noise.
