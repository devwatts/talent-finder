About Tiberius Aerospace

Tiberius Aerospace is a cutting-edge aerospace and defense company delivering advanced, mission-critical technologies for government and private-sector customers. The company develops high-performance aerospace systems, secure engineering solutions, and complex hardware for demanding operational environments.

Position Overview

We are seeking a driven Technical Recruiter to help build the engineering and technical teams at Tiberius Aerospace. This role will manage full-cycle recruiting for specialized positions across aerospace, defense, engineering, and advanced manufacturing.

Reporting to Steven Brown, Senior Staff Recruiter, you will partner with hiring managers to translate technical requirements into targeted searches, engage exceptional candidates, and manage an efficient hiring process. The successful candidate will bring 3-5 years of technical recruiting experience, including required experience recruiting within the defense industry, along with the ability to assess technical backgrounds and build relationships with specialized talent.

What You’ll Do


Manage full-cycle technical recruiting, including intake meetings, sourcing, screening, interview coordination, offer support, and candidate closing
Partner with the Senior Staff Recruiter and hiring managers to define position requirements, hiring priorities, target companies, and candidate assessment criteria
Recruit across mechanical engineering, structural analysis, aerodynamics, computational fluid dynamics (CFD), propulsion, guidance, navigation and control (GNC), embedded software, electrical engineering, systems engineering, test, and manufacturing
Develop targeted talent maps and candidate pipelines across defense contractors, aerospace manufacturers, emerging defense technology companies, and specialized engineering organizations
Identify and engage passive candidates through LinkedIn Recruiter, Boolean search, referrals, professional networks, and direct research
Conduct substantive screening conversations to evaluate technical qualifications, hands-on ownership, career motivations, and alignment with position requirements
Present clear candidate summaries outlining qualifications, technical strengths, compensation expectations, location, availability, and areas requiring further assessment
Coordinate interviews, gather timely feedback, and maintain consistent communication with candidates and hiring teams
Maintain accurate applicant tracking records and provide visibility into pipeline health, search progress, and hiring challenges
Support offer development, negotiations, and closing in partnership with the Senior Staff Recruiter and appropriate internal stakeholders
Work with HR, legal, and security teams to follow applicable hiring, export-control, and clearance-related procedures
Represent Tiberius Aerospace with professionalism, discretion, and a strong commitment to the candidate experience



What You’ll Bring


3-5 years of technical recruiting experience
Prior experience recruiting within the defense industry is required, either in-house or through a recruiting firm supporting defense clients
Demonstrated success sourcing and hiring engineers for specialized, difficult-to-fill positions
Working knowledge of aerospace and defense engineering disciplines and the ability to discuss technical requirements credibly with candidates and hiring managers
Proficiency with LinkedIn Recruiter, advanced Boolean search, talent mapping, and passive candidate engagement
Experience managing multiple searches while maintaining clear priorities, accurate documentation, and consistent follow-through
Familiarity with recruiting for positions involving security-clearance requirements and export-controlled work
Strong judgment when evaluating candidate qualifications and distinguishing direct technical experience from adjacent exposure
Excellent communication, organizational, and relationship-management skills
Ability to work effectively in a fast-moving environment with evolving hiring needs
U.S. citizenship and eligibility to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance



Nice to Have


Experience recruiting for missile systems, guided weapons, propulsion, hypersonics, autonomous systems, or advanced defense hardware
Experience supporting a startup or high-growth aerospace and defense company
An established network within Southern California’s defense and aerospace engineering community
Experience recruiting structural analysts, aerodynamicists, propulsion engineers, GNC engineers, and embedded software engineers
Experience supporting cleared hiring and coordinating with personnel security teams
Familiarity with recruiting analytics and responsible use of automation to improve sourcing and execution
Bachelor’s degree in Human Resources, Business, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience
